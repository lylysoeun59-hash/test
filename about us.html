What Makes Us Special

At Grand Hotel, we combine authentic local hospitality with modern luxury standards. Nestled in a prime location, our property offers serene tropical landscapes, hand-crafted interior architecture, and a peaceful escape while keeping you just minutes away from major city landmarks and nightlife destinations.

Gourmet Dining & Breakfast

Start your mornings with our complimentary buffet featuring a rich blend of traditional Khmer dishes and international Western breakfasts. Our on-site restaurant serves fresh organic meals prepared by top local chefs throughout the day.

Wellness & Luxury Spa

Rejuvenate your body and mind at our full-service spa center. Choose from traditional Khmer herbal massages, oil aromatherapy, body scrubs, and sauna sessions designed to melt away your travel stress.

Guided Tours & Local Guides

Explore regional temples, historic landmarks, and hidden local gems with our certified multi-lingual tour guides. We organize personalized private day tours and group excursions customized to your schedule.

Motorbike & Bicycle Rentals

Explore the town on your own schedule! We offer reliable automatic motorbikes, city bicycles, and helmet gear for daily or hourly rentals directly from our front desk with free road assistance.

Rooftop Lounge & Club Partnerships

Enjoy sunset cocktails at our exclusive hotel rooftop lounge. Guests also receive VIP access, free entry tickets, and drink discounts at our partner nightlife clubs and music venues across the city.
